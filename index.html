See your real position in the market.

JPMAPEX is not a brokerage, a wallet, or a trading platform. It is a private journal and analytics tool that runs entirely on your device. The result is the kind of record that turns decisions into evidence.

  • Native SwiftUI rendering — no third-party chart libraries.
  • No telemetry, analytics, or background network calls.
  • Tags by strategy, asset class, and intent.
  • Export everything; reset everything; you stay in control.
