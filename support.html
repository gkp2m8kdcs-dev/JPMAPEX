Frequently asked questions

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. JPMAPEX is private by design. All trades, holdings, notes, and attachments are stored locally in the app's sandbox. See the Privacy Policy.

Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts, no logins, and no sign-up.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Use the export action in Settings to produce a copy of your transactions and holdings. You can also reset everything or delete the app to remove all local data.

Does JPMAPEX connect to my brokerage?

No. JPMAPEX is a journal and analytics tool. You enter your trades manually, attach notes and receipts, and the app computes your portfolio from those entries. It is not a brokerage, exchange, wallet, or trading platform.

Where do market quotes come from?

When you open a market detail screen, the app may fetch a public quote from a third-party market data endpoint over HTTPS to draw the chart. These requests contain no personal data and no analytics payload. The app is fully usable offline — remote charts will simply show their last cached value.

Does the app show ads or sell my data?

No. There are no ads, no tracking, and no analytics SDKs.

Can I use JPMAPEX for tax records?

JPMAPEX keeps a detailed audit trail of every trade with date, price, quantity, fees, and notes, and you can produce a CSV export. That said, the app does not provide tax advice and you should consult a qualified professional for tax filing.

How do I delete everything?

Open Settings inside JPMAPEX and tap Reset to clear all data, or uninstall the app to remove everything along with its sandbox.

How do I report a bug?

Email datltan1991@icloud.com with a description, steps to reproduce, and screenshots if possible. We will get back to you.
