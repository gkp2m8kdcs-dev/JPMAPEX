Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Short version: JPMAPEX is private by design. There are no accounts, no logins, no servers, no analytics, and no advertising. All your trades, holdings, notes, and attachments live in a sandbox on your iPhone. You can export, reset, or delete everything at any time.

1. Who we are

JPMAPEX ("the App", "we", "us") is a personal investment journal and analytics tool published under the App Store listing name "JPMAPEX". For any privacy-related question you can reach us at datltan1991@icloud.com.

2. Data the app stores on your device

The App stores the following data locally on your device, inside its sandbox:

  • Transactions you record (symbol, asset class, price, quantity, fees, dates, notes).
  • Holdings, portfolio cost basis, and computed analytics derived from your transactions.
  • Attachments you add to a trade (photos and files you choose to attach).
  • App preferences such as display currency and theme.

This data is yours. It is not transmitted to us or to any third party by the App. Removing the App deletes this data.

3. Data we do NOT collect

We do not collect, transmit, or have access to:

  • Your name, email, phone number, or any contact information.
  • Your Apple ID, iCloud account, or any sign-in credential.
  • Device identifiers, advertising identifiers (IDFA), or advertising profiles.
  • Location data, contacts, calendar, microphone, camera (beyond the photo picker you explicitly open), or biometrics.
  • Crash logs, analytics events, telemetry, or usage statistics.
  • Browsing history inside the app — because the App has no browser.

4. Permissions

The App intentionally avoids permission prompts. It does not request:

  • Notifications
  • Location services
  • Contacts
  • Face ID / Touch ID
  • Camera roll read access (attachments are added via the standard Photos picker, which does not require full library access)
  • Tracking (App Tracking Transparency is not triggered)

5. Network

The App does not initiate background network calls. When you open a market index detail screen, the App may fetch a public quote from a third-party market data endpoint over HTTPS to display a chart. These requests contain no personal data, no device identifier, and no analytics payload — only the market symbol being viewed. You can use the App fully offline; charts that depend on remote quotes will simply show their last cached value or a "data unavailable" state.

6. Third-party services

The App does not embed third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or social SDKs. If a future version adds an optional cloud backup feature, this policy will be revised and your affirmative opt-in will be required before any data leaves your device.

7. Children

The App is rated 4+. It does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

8. Your rights and controls

Because the data lives only on your device, all rights are local:

  • Export — use the in-app export to produce a copy of your data.
  • Reset — clear all transactions, holdings, and attachments from Settings.
  • Delete — uninstalling the App removes all local data.

If you have any privacy request that goes beyond local controls (for example because a future feature was added that we operate on your behalf), email datltan1991@icloud.com and we will respond within 30 days.

9. International users

The App runs entirely on your device and does not transfer your personal data across borders, because we do not have it. This applies under EU GDPR, Brazil LGPD, California CCPA/CPRA, and other regional privacy frameworks.

10. Changes to this policy

If we update this policy we will post the revised text at this URL and update the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will also be surfaced in the App's Settings screen on the next launch.

11. Contact

Privacy questions: datltan1991@icloud.com
